# Product

**User.** Browser incremental-game players (fans of *Universal Paperclips*, *Cookie Clicker* and *A Dark Room*) who like satire about AI and big tech. Also people curious about the attention-economy and AI-race arguments who will play for 30 minutes but won't read an essay.

**Painful job.** "Give me a short incremental game with an idea in it, and with an ending worth reaching and showing off."

**Promise.** Run the company eating the attention economy, or defect and race it to superintelligence. Ten endings, about half an hour for a first run, free in your browser.

**Wedge.**
- Ending cards ("I reached THE CONVERSION — 2 of 10 endings found") and `?ending=` links that show a friend which ending you reached.
- A guide to all ten endings for the "game name + all endings" search.
- Searches for "games like Universal Paperclips".
- GitHub incremental-game topics.

**Why now.** The AI race and the attention economy are the argument of the moment. Incremental-game audiences share their endings.

**Activation metric.** An ending reached (`ending_*`). The swing factor is shares per ending. Both count once the GoatCounter key is set.

**First revenue event.** A pay-what-you-want payment on an itch.io page through the footer support link (OPERATIONS.md keys 2 and 4).

**What v1 deliberately won't do.**
- No real-world calls to action, no links to real organizations, no accounts and no leaderboard.
- No paid tier, no ads and no cookies.
- No extra endings or lore pages beyond the merged ten.
