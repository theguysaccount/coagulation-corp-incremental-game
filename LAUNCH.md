### Drafts

**r/incremental_games:** "Coagulation Corp — a two-act satirical incremental: run the attention-economy megacorp, or defect and race it to superintelligence (10 endings, free, no account)"
> Act I is a classic tiered incremental: attention → capital → compute, with combo, overclock, signals and prestige. A coherence meter quietly bleeds as you scale. Act II starts when you take The Defection. You found the rival lab and race your old company, and research alone can't close the gap; you have to bring their people over, which only sticks if your org stays coherent. When and how you defect shapes the race. A first run is about 20–40 minutes. Ten endings, each with a shareable card. Feedback on pacing and the defection timing is very welcome.

**Show HN:** "Show HN: Coagulation Corp — Universal Paperclips, but you can defect"
> Two vanilla-JS incrementals merged into one static site. Scripted players run the real game code at every deploy to keep pacing inside a 20–40 minute first run. Endings, cards and saves stay in the browser.
